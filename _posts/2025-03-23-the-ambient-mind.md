date: 2025-03-23
---

The mind works best when it isn't forced. Ideas arrive not when we stare at a blank page, but when we're walking, showering, or half-asleep. This ambient mode of thinking—where attention drifts and connections form on their own—is where creativity lives. We chase it by making space, not by filling every minute with tasks.
